✦ OUR LEGACY ✦

The Original Storefront

What started as a small, humble kitchen in 1960 has grown into a beloved Bengaluru landmark, while maintaining the exact recipes established over six decades ago.

Three generations of passion, dedication, and unwavering commitment to authentic taste have made Aramane Sweets synonymous with quality and heritage in Karnataka.
